Medical Doctor / Specialist

Conduct in-clinic consultations, manage structured visit notes with AI summary review, issue prescriptions, and review diagnostic lab reports.

  • MD / MBBS with active medical license
  • Board certification or specialization
  • Clinical empathy and collaborative practice

Clinic Receptionist

Manage front-desk patient arrivals, schedule conflict-free appointments, verify intake documents, and coordinate billing invoices.

  • High school diploma or Associate degree
  • Strong customer service & organization
  • Proficiency with digital booking systems

Laboratory Technician

Receive lab orders, handle specimen collection, perform diagnostic tests, and upload laboratory results for physician verification.

  • MLT / MLS certification or related degree
  • Experience in clinical hematology & chemistry
  • Strict adherence to laboratory safety protocols

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn about our clinic operations, in-person appointments, record security, and AI scope.

Patients can browse clinic departments, select a doctor, and pick an open time slot. The system validates live doctor availability and clinic room allocation in real time, preventing double bookings and ensuring a smooth, scheduled in-person visit.
AI in MedAssist is strictly limited to exactly two non-diagnostic administrative features used after an in-person visit: (1) converting a doctor's structured visit notes into a concise clinical summary for the doctor to review and approve, and (2) explaining doctor-issued prescriptions and follow-up instructions in plain language for the patient. AI never diagnoses, never triages symptoms, and never recommends specialists.
During your in-person visit, your attending physician enters official digital prescriptions and diagnostic lab orders directly into the EMR. Lab technicians collect samples at the clinic and enter findings, which are verified by your doctor before being released to your patient timeline.
Medical doctors, receptionists, and lab technicians can apply directly through our Careers section. Applications create a StaffApplication record in our database, which is reviewed by clinic administrators before setting up staff credentials.
Yes. MedAssist is designed with enterprise security standards adhering to HIPAA regulations. Patient EMR data, appointment logs, visit notes, lab results, and invoices are protected with role-based access controls and encrypted data transmission.
